# Changes in v2 (Duel Mode revision)

| # | Request | What changed | How it is checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every card unique; new effect types; highlight the Graveyard when it holds usable effects | All 60 cards were redesigned in `tools/design_cards.py`: each has its own cost/stats/effect combination (48 different effect building blocks in use, 9 timings). New effects include destroying/revealing Traps, blocking Traps, negating a summon effect or a Spell, freeze, silence, revival from either Graveyard, "when destroyed" and "when milled" triggers, Graveyard-activated effects, coin flips, random picks, sacrifice and pay-LP trades. A second card named "The Evening Star" was renamed "The Final Course". The Graveyard pile pulses gold (with a count badge) when an effect can be used now; inside, usable cards are marked READY with a *Use effect* button. | `dev_checks`: `cards_unique` (60/60 distinct), one check per new effect family |
| 2 | More low-cost cards | In v1 a card's Insight cost was recomputed from its stats and **rose with its level**: Monsters cost 3-6 at Level 1 (none below 3), 4-7 at Level 2 and 5-9 at Level 3 (Insight tops out at 10). Cost is now fixed data per card, the same at every level, from 1 to 6: 8 cards cost 1, 15 cost 2, 20 cost 3, 11 cost 4, 4 cost 5 and 2 cost 6 (23 of 60 cost 2 or less). Mean Monster ATK+HP by cost: 1: 3.0, 2: 3.8, 3: 4.9, 4: 6.0, 5: 7.8, 6: 10.5. | `cost_curve` check: at least 20 cards cost <= 2, at least 5 one-cost Monsters, mean stats strictly rise with cost |
| 3 | Exact numbers in card text | Card text is generated from the same data as the rules, per level (e.g. "Deal 3 damage to the enemy's face."). The vague glossary lines are gone; the *Effects* screen is now a keyword glossary. | `text_exact` check: every number of every ability appears in the text of all 60 cards at all 3 levels, and the old vague phrases never do |
| 4 | Master Duel-style animations | The engine can record events (draw, summon, spell, Trap flip, effect, attack, damage, destroy, banish, to-Graveyard, coin flip, negate, turn start...). `src/ui/anim.cpp` replays them: cards fly between zones, effect banners show the card and its text, attacks lunge, destroyed cards shatter, banished cards spiral into the banish pile, coins spin (also at the opening toss), LP counts down. The opponent's turn is played the same way instead of the "what did the opponent do" pop-up; the History log still records everything. **Space** skips, the **Anim** button sets Normal / Fast / Off. | `events_recorded` check; `stress_test` verifies the last event's snapshot equals the real table in every turn of hundreds of matches |
| 5 | Readable fonts/colours | Text on coloured buttons is chosen from the button colour (WCAG contrast): the "ready" line on the green Duel button was 1.8:1 and is now 6.5:1. Trap-type colour 3.8:1 -> 6.2:1, muted text and borders brightened, the Good button darkened, small card text enlarged, the school watermark behind card text made fainter, floating numbers get a drop shadow. | contrast ratios computed from the palette; screenshots at 1920x1080 |
| 6 | Resolution | The UI no longer renders a fixed 1280x720 picture that is stretched to the window. It draws at the window's real pixel size with a uniform scale, fonts are rasterised at the exact pixel size they are drawn at, mouse input is mapped through the same transform, and the window opens at a size that fits the monitor. F11 = borderless fullscreen, `--size WxH` to choose. | screenshots at 1280x720, 1920x1080 and 2560x1080 |

Also fixed: `third_party/nlohmann/json.hpp` was the multi-header entry point (needs ~40 files that were not in the zip), so a clean build failed;
it is now the single-header release. Balance note: with the richer effects the Hard bot beats the Easy bot 35 matches to 5 (v1: 40 to 0).
Not tested here: Windows/macOS builds (the CMake file is unchanged apart from the new test target).
